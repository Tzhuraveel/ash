# Ash

Ash is a free Telegram Mini App and bot that tracks appointment slot ("талон") availability for the practical driving exam at Ukrainian service centers (СЦ / сервісний центр) and notifies you the moment a new slot opens up.

## Why

Service centers publish talon availability, but there's no way to subscribe to changes — you have to keep checking manually. A paid Telegram bot already solves this, but it costs money. Ash is a free, open-source alternative, built as a pet project to learn along the way.

## Scope

Practical driving exam only for now — other talon types (theory exam, document services, etc.) may follow later.

## Features

- Browse available talon dates and times by category and service center
- Subscribe to a service center + category and get a Telegram notification the moment a new slot opens up
- See all your active subscriptions and their current availability in one place, and unsubscribe anytime
- Jump straight to the service center's official booking page to grab the slot
